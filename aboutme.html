About Me

My journey in Library and Information Science began with a deep curiosity about how knowledge is preserved and shared within communities. I believe libraries are more than collections of books — they are spaces that empower people through access to reliable information.

As a student, I strive to develop strong skills in archival management, preservation, and information literacy. My goal is to contribute to strengthening community libraries and protecting local history for future generations.

Skills

  • Organizing and cataloging archival materials
  • Basic preservation techniques for historical documents
  • Research and information retrieval
  • Information literacy support and user assistance
  • Communication and teamwork in academic settings

Interests

I am particularly interested in community libraries and local archives, especially initiatives that help preserve regional history. I am also curious about how technology can improve access to information and make library services more inclusive and accessible.

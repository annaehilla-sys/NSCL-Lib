About Me

My journey in Library and Information Science began with a deep curiosity about how knowledge is preserved and shared within communities.


I believe libraries are more than collections of books — they are spaces that empower people through access to reliable information.

As a student, I continue to develop strong skills in archival management, preservation, and information literacy. My goal is to contribute to strengthening community libraries and protecting local history for future generations.

Core Skills

  • Organizing and cataloging archival materials
  • Preservation techniques for historical documents
  • Research and information retrieval
  • Information literacy support
  • Academic communication and teamwork

Interests

  • Community libraries and local archives
  • Preserving regional and cultural history
  • Using technology to improve access to information
  • Inclusive and accessible library services

Hobbies

  • Reading fiction and educational books
  • Exploring digital tools and creative design
  • Visiting libraries and cultural spaces
  • Organizing personal collections
  • Watching documentaries

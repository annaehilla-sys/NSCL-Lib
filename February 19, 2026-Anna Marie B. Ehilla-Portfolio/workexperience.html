Work Experience

I have 3 years of experience as a Student Assistant at Noguchi Library, specializing in the preservation and conservation of rare books and archival materials. I supported handling, basic conservation treatments, cataloging, and assisted researchers using the Filipiniana collection.

Responsibilities

  • Handle and prepare fragile materials for safe use and storage.
  • Assist with basic conservation treatments and stabilization of damaged items.
  • Rehouse and stabilize collections to reduce further deterioration.
  • Catalog and update metadata for special-collection items to improve discoverability.
  • Support researchers with access to archival materials and maintain handling protocols.

Achievements

  • Stabilized and rehoused over 120 fragile volumes, reducing handling damage and improving shelf safety.
  • Updated metadata for 200+ Filipiniana items, improving discoverability for researchers.
  • Contributed to a small digitization pilot that preserved several fragile pamphlets for long-term access.

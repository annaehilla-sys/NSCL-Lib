About Us

Noguchi Library serves as the Filipiniana collection library of Father Saturnino Urios University, dedicated to preserving and promoting local history, culture, and scholarly research.

The library is named after Prof. Takashi Noguchi, a Japanese researcher who devoted his academic career to studying Philippine history. He donated Filipiniana books and research materials to help preserve and promote local information for future generations.

Opened on June 6, 2013, the library now holds 2,379 titles and 3,766 volumes and continues to grow through yearly donations and collection development.

Vision

To preserve, conserve, promote, and strengthen Filipiniana collections in the region and in the Philippines.

Mission

Father Saturnino Urios University Research Library exists to preserve and promote local knowledge through instruction and research.

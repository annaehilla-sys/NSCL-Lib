Welcome to Noguchi Library

The Noguchi Library is the Filipiniana collection library of Father Saturnino Urios University, dedicated to preserving local history, culture, and scholarly research. It provides access to valuable books, journals, and historical materials to support academic learning and cultural awareness.

Explore our collections, research services, and resources designed to support students, faculty, and visiting researchers.

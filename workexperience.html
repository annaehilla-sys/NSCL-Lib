Work Experience

As a Student Assistant at Noguchi Library, I gained hands-on experience in archival preservation and special collections work.


This role allowed me to apply classroom knowledge in a real-world setting, strengthen my attention to detail, and develop a deep appreciation for the responsible stewardship of rare and historical materials.

Key Responsibilities

  • Handled and prepared fragile materials for safe use and storage.
  • Assisted with conservation treatments and stabilization of damaged items.
  • Rehoused collections to reduce further deterioration.
  • Cataloged and updated metadata for special collections.
  • Supported researchers while maintaining proper handling protocols.

Achievements

  • Stabilized and rehoused over 120 fragile volumes.
  • Updated metadata for Filipiniana books.
  • Contributed to a digitization pilot preserving fragile pamphlets.

On The Job Training (OJT)

  • Sorted and organized documents at the Property Maintenance Office.
  • Managed and filed Purchase Orders efficiently.
  • Maintained a systematic filing system for easy retrieval.
  • Successfully organized over 150 Purchase Orders.
  • Streamlined document retrieval process for staff.

Work Experience

Through my role as a Student Assistant at Noguchi Library, I gained hands-on experience in archival preservation and special collections work. This position allowed me to apply classroom knowledge in a real-world setting, strengthen my attention to detail, and develop a deep appreciation for responsible stewardship of rare and historical materials.

Responsibilities

  • Handled and prepared fragile materials for safe use and storage.
  • Assisted with basic conservation treatments and stabilization of damaged items.
  • Rehoused and stabilized collections to reduce further deterioration.
  • Cataloged and updated metadata for special-collection items to improve discoverability.
  • Supported researchers with access to archival materials while maintaining handling protocols.

Achievements

  • Stabilized and rehoused over 120 fragile volumes, improving shelf safety.
  • Updated metadata for 200+ Filipiniana items, increasing discoverability.
  • Contributed to a digitization pilot preserving fragile pamphlets for long-term access.
